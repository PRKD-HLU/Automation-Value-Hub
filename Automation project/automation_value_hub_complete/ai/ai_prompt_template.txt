Prepare a concise monthly management summary for the Automation Value & Reliability Hub.

Rules:
1. Use only the values and statements contained in the supplied JSON.
2. Do not calculate or invent missing values.
3. Do not claim a root cause unless it is explicitly present in the data.
4. Clearly distinguish measured, reconstructed and estimated information.
5. Mention data-quality limitations and missing reporting.
6. Structure the response as:
   - Executive Summary
   - Key Results
   - Automations Requiring Attention
   - Recommended Follow-up
7. Keep the tone professional and suitable for a manager.
8. Limit the output to approximately 250 words.
